### The matrix (164 cells)

| part | cells |
|---|---|
| codec: pac-zstd (248 kB blocks), zstd3 and zstd19 (hdf5plugin, 256 kB), pac-lz4 (56 kB), lz4 (hdf5plugin, 8 kB) x data: silicon, ceo2, thaumatin x u16/u32 x mask0/maskmax-1 x mode: sync, async (2 contexts alternated, 4 calls per repetition), device (LZ4 only) | 144 |
| synthetic twins (`$SLABITZ_SYNTH/twins`): silicon and ceo2 x zstd/lz4 x mask0/maskmax, thaumatin lz4 | 9 |
| T00 worst cases: min_matches, short_offsets_1_31 (`.zst`), random_u16, all_max_u16, all_max_u32 (`.zst`, zstd .h5, lz4 .h5); repeated to 16 frames | 11 |

16 frames per call, 16 distinct frames evenly spaced through each real file; the input is
encoded once per (group, codec), outside every timed loop. The expected output is the source
pixels (real), the h5py/hdf5plugin read (twins, worst .h5) or `raw.bin` (worst .zst).

Per cell:
- **Wall time:** median and IQR of 9 repetitions, after an adaptive warmup.
- **Device time:** per kernel, from SLABITZ_PROFILE=1. It is the median over 5 fresh contexts
  of each context's mean over 2 calls.
- **Compared bytes:** every warm context's output is read back after the warmup and compared
  with the CPU decode.

## Decisions

1. **Block sizes.**
   - pac: its defaults, PAC_ZSTD_BLOCK 253952 and PAC_LZ4_BLOCK 57344.
   - Stock zstd: 256 kB, as the ESRF zstd files.
   - Stock LZ4: the bitshuffle default of 8 kB, as the detector writes.
2. **Distinct frames, not one tiled frame** (pac_matrix tiles one). The input build costs about
   10 s per group (zstd19 on CeO2 u32); a whole run takes 187 s.
3. **Async and device repetitions.**
   - One repetition is 4 calls alternated over two contexts, with the next call queued before
     the other context is waited for. The time per call is the repetition time / 4.
   - Device mode uploads the input once, outside the loop.
4. **Profile.**
   - The rows `alloc`, `free`, `sync`, `upload`, `download` and `fill32` are recorded but not
     gated: they are host-timed or first-call operations, not kernels.
   - `fill32` and `download` varied by up to 7x between identical runs.
5. **Kernel rule.** A kernel fails when it is more than 20% slower *and* more than 20 us
   slower per call. The task said "more than 10% slower and above 20 us": the 20 us is read as
   the difference, and the 20% is a deviation (see below).
6. **Idle guard.**
   - It samples nvidia-smi 3 times over 1 s for our GPU (matched by PCI bus id) and excludes
     the gate's own pid.
   - The closing check waits 3 s first, because the utilisation counter lags and showed the
     gate's own last cells as 100%.
   - A run that is busy at either end is marked `not_a_baseline`; `--write-baseline` refuses
     it, and refuses `--only`, other frame counts and selftest runs.
7. **zstd cells capped at the decoder's 1 GiB request limit.**
   - The limit is `kzstdgpu_MaxRequestUncompressedByteSize`.
   - thaumatin u32 is 16 x 72.4 MB = 1.16 GB per call, so its 12 zstd cells run 14 frames.
   - The cell records `frames`; LZ4 cells keep 16.
8. **`CUDA_MODULE_LOADING=EAGER`, set by gate.py before cuInit.**
   - With lazy loading, the profiled ParseCompressedBlocks of a fresh context read 425-470 us
     instead of 40-46 us, in some processes and cells only.
   - With eager loading it is steady. Wall times are unaffected either way, since they use
     warm contexts.
9. **Adaptive warmup.**
   - At least 2 warmup calls, then more (up to 10) until two in a row agree within 3%.
   - The 1.16 GB LZ4 cells kept getting faster for about 6 calls
     (`lz4/thaumatin_u32_maskmax-1/sync` 18.8 -> 8.5 ms).
   - The noise run's largest ratio went from 1.238 to 1.058 with this change.
10. **Results directory.**
    - In the root it is `$SLABITZ_DEV_ROOT/results/`; elsewhere `bench/results/` (gitignored).
    - `SLABITZ_GATE_RESULTS` overrides both.
11. **`--burn S`** loads the GPU by decoding a synthetic chunk in a loop, so it needs no other
    CUDA code.

## Deviations from the task file

- **Compare is pinned to the baseline's node.**
  - Two L40S nodes are not interchangeable. Measured against the frcrg-gpu5l40s-01 baseline,
    a run on gpbm18-34 (job 38880796) was 8-16% slower in 20 cells, most of them LZ4 sync and
    worst-case cells.
  - `--compare` fails when the node differs, unless `--any-node` is given.
    `--baseline-node` prints the node for `sbatch -w`.
  - AGENTS.md and the reviewer step in docs/dev/README.md give the command. README.md is not in
    "Files that may change"; the reviewer step had to change with the gate.
- **Kernel threshold 20%, not 10%.**
  - On one node and one commit, `DecompressSequencesMultiStream` of
    `zstd19/silicon_u16_mask0` was 195 us in one process and 219 us in another (+12%).
    `zstd3/silicon_u16_mask0/async` went 431 -> 475 us.
  - The value was steady within each process, so a 10% rule failed acceptance 4 on an
    unchanged commit (job 38880795). The constant is `KERNEL_SLOWER`.
  - **Needs the maintainer's acceptance.**
- **More reps per cell than the minimum.** 9 reps, as asked; the warmup is adaptive (decision 9).

## Open issues

- **The gate's real sensitivity differs per cell.**
  - The wall rule (more than 5% and more than 3 IQR) is tight for most cells, but the noisiest
    are only loosely gated.
  - IQR/median in the baseline: `zstd3/thaumatin_u16_mask0/async` 0.30,
    `pac-zstd/silicon_u16_maskmax-1/async` 0.17, `lz4/thaumatin_u32_maskmax-1/sync` 0.14,
    `worst/all_max_u32_lz4_8k/sync` 0.14 (a 26 us cell).
  - Between processes, the LZ4 CeO2 sync cells moved 5-10% (compare job: ratios 1.03-1.10)
    and passed because of their IQR.
  - A regression smaller than about 10% in those cells can pass. T04 could find out why the
    async cells and the CeO2 LZ4 cells vary.
- **`--noise` runs both passes in one process**, so it does not see the between-process
  variation above. The compare against the baseline (a separate process) is the real test.
- **Decoder reject mismatch (for T09).** A zstd call over the 1 GiB request limit returns
  `SBZ_ERR_CORRUPT` ("frame 0: status 0xa07a0006", RequestTooLarge), not `SBZ_ERR_LIMIT`. The
  host could check the sum of content sizes before launching.
- **Performance observations for T04/T21, from the baseline.**
  - LZ4 device input is slower than async host input for stock LZ4 (8 kB blocks):
    silicon u32 mask0 0.58 ms async vs 0.90 ms device; thaumatin u32 mask0 2.99 vs 3.96 ms.
  - It is 3-4x faster on CeO2 (lz4 ceo2_u16_mask0 3.18 vs 0.81 ms).
  - `worst/min_matches` takes 15.1 ms for 2 MB (DecompressSequencesMultiStream 7.9 ms,
    ExecuteSequences 7.0 ms).
  - `worst/short_offsets_1_31` takes 1.56 ms (ExecuteSequences 1.3 ms).
  - Lazy module loading makes a new context's first ParseCompressedBlocks about 400 us slower
    (users that create a context per request pay it).
- **`sbz build` is not safe in two jobs at once on one worktree.** Two gate jobs started
  together; one failed at `ln: failed to create symbolic link 'build/sbz_kernels.id': File
  exists` (job 38880950). Chain jobs with `--dependency` (done here) or fix the link step in
  `tools/sbz.sh` (T-1 tooling).
- **Baselines for the GTX 1050 Ti and TITAN Black** are for the user, and they need a decision.
  - The thaumatin u32 LZ4 cells need 1.16 GB of output per call plus the input. That is more
    than a 4 GB card can hold together with the decoder's buffers.
  - `--write-baseline` requires 16 frames, so old cards cannot write a baseline today.
  - Options: let `--write-baseline` accept `--frames N` and put N in the baseline's name
    (e.g. `gtx-1050-ti-f4.json`), or skip the largest cells on small cards.
  - Not done: question for the maintainer.
